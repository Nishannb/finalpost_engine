# CaptionKit font licences

Every font in this folder is free for commercial use, embedding and redistribution under the licence listed below.
The OFL files carry each family's copyright line (read from the font's own `name` table) above the full SIL Open Font
License 1.1 text. The three Apache 2.0 families share `LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt`; their copyright notices are listed in
`NOTICE-Apache-fonts.txt`.

Neither licence lets the fonts be sold on their own. OFL Reserved Font Names must not be used for modified versions.
Rendering captions with them into a user's video places no licence condition on the video.
